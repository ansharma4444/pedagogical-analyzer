### 1. Grade 1 — Foundational Phonics (ABC Mouse, Target Grade: 1)
> The cat is on the mat. The cat has a red hat. Can you see the cat? A is for apple. B is for ball. Tap the letter A! A-A-Apple! Great job! You are a superstar reader.

### 2. Grade 2 — Basic Storytelling (Epic! Books, Target Grade: 2)
> Once there was a little blue bird. He wanted to fly high in the sky. He flapped his wings. He went up, up, up! He saw the green trees. He saw the yellow sun. It was a very happy day for the little bird.

### 3. Grade 6 — General Science (BrainPOP, Target Grade: 6)
> The water cycle is the path that all water follows as it moves around Earth. When the sun heats up water in rivers or lakes, it turns into vapor. This is called evaporation. When the vapor cools down, it forms clouds. This is called condensation.

### 4. Grade 8 — Civic Studies (Civics 101, Target Grade: 8)
> The Constitution is the highest law in the land. It explains how the government is organized and how it works. It also protects the rights of all citizens. The first ten changes to the Constitution are called the Bill of Rights.

### 5. Grade 9 — World History (NCERT History, Target Grade: 9)
> The French Revolution began in 1789. It was a period of social and political upheaval in France. The revolution overthrew the monarchy and established a republic based on the ideas of liberty, equality, and fraternity.

### 6. Grade 10 — Chemistry Basics (BYJU'S, Target Grade: 10)
> Chemical reactions involve the breaking and forming of bonds between atoms. In an exothermic reaction, energy is released into the surroundings, usually as heat or light. Combustion is a common example of an exothermic process.

### 7. Grade 12 — Advanced Physics (Tiwari Academy, Target Grade: 12)
> Electromagnetic induction is the production of an electromotive force across an electrical conductor in a changing magnetic field. This phenomenon is mathematically described by Faraday's Law and is the basis for electric generators.

### 8. Grade 12+ — Computer Science (Coursera Data Science, Target Grade: 12)
> Linear regression is a statistical method used to model the relationship between a dependent variable and one or more independent variables. We use the method of least squares to minimize the sum of the squares of the vertical deviations.

### 9. TEST CASE — Misaligned (Too Hard) ("Science for Kids", Target Grade: 3)
> The atmospheric pressure exerted by the gaseous envelope surrounding the Earth is measured using a barometric instrument. This pressure fluctuates based on altitude and thermal variations within the troposphere.

*(This is Grade 12 vocabulary shown to a Grade 3 audience — the tool should report a very low Alignment Score here. This is the best single example to lead with in a live demo.)*
